===== page 3 =====
• Coreform Cubit is a semi-
automated hex-mesher
• Means that Cubit can recognize 
certain topologies as meshable 
without further decomposition by the 
user
• A "many-to-one sweep" (N:1) is 
one of the techniques that can 
recognize topologies as meshable
• Importantly, whenever an N:1 
sweep is used, you could have done 
additional decompositions to make 
multiple 1:1 sweeps
